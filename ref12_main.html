Hidden Jungle Clearing!

The long lost off-the-beaten-track Jungle clearing, that contains a small collection of games that didn’t quite fit anywhere else. They’re not CRASH Smashes, they’re not by Ultimate, and they’re not titles I spent my youth glued to… but they’re far too good, or iconic to be left out in the cold.

Inside this clearing you’ll find a small sample of my personal favourite retail releases, homebrew gems, and other titles that may not necessarily be the best example of gaming code ever written, but are none-the-less an important part of Spectrum gaming history.

Started by Michael Winn, 2001
Completed and updated, 2026
