THE WILD BUNCH

PUBLISHER
Firebird

AUTHOR
Kevin Smith

YEAR
1984

DESCRIPTION
Menu-based text adventure based on the Wild West

CONTROLS:
Simply input options as prompted on the main screen

INSTRUCTIONS
A man has been shot and you were discovered on the scene.  You
manage to take a note of the description of the real killer, and
make a getaway before being arrested.  But the sheriff has put
a Pinkerton Agent on your trail, so you must find and arrest the
real killer before the agent arrests you.  You must keep your
strength up by buying equipment and drinking in the tavern.

CHEATS
With the use of snapshotting available on the emulators, it is
easy enough to challenge everybody, and if they turn out to be
innocent, simply reload the saved snapshot. It the are one of the
wild Bunch, shoot `em!!

SEQUELS
None

NOTES
Original menu based adventure which although it can be repetitive,
is still quite enjoyable today.
