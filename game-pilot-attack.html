Pilot Attack

Created by:Mika Keranen
Price:Free
Year:2017
Source:zxart.ee

Pilot Attack is a horizontal shoot 'em up game developed by Misfit and released in 2017 for the ZX Spectrum 48K. The game is set in a space environment where players control a spacecraft navigating through various obstacles and enemies. The objective is to survive and score points by defeating enemy ships.

The game features a retro-style design, typical of ZX Spectrum games, with colourful graphics and a straightforward control scheme. Players must manage their shield and fuel levels while avoiding or destroying incoming threats. Published by The Future Was 8 Bit, it offers a nostalgic experience for fans of classic shooters.
